==============================================
         PAKISTAN BASIC INFORMATION
==============================================

----------------------------------------------
GENERAL INFO
----------------------------------------------
Official Name: Islamic Republic of Pakistan
Capital: Islamabad
Largest City: Karachi
National Language: Urdu
Official Languages: Urdu, English
Currency: Pakistani Rupee (PKR)
Independence: 14 August 1947
Founder: Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah
National Poet: Allama Muhammad Iqbal

----------------------------------------------
GEOGRAPHY
----------------------------------------------
Location: South Asia
Area: 881,913 km² (33rd largest)
Borders:
  - India (East)
  - Afghanistan (West/North)
  - Iran (Southwest)
  - China (Northeast)
Coastline: Arabian Sea (South)
Highest Point: K2 (8,611m) - 2nd highest in world
Major Rivers: Indus, Jhelum, Chenab, Ravi, Sutlej

----------------------------------------------
POPULATION (2024)
----------------------------------------------
Total Population: ~240 Million
World Rank: 5th most populous
Growth Rate: ~2%
Urban Population: ~37%
Literacy Rate: ~62%
